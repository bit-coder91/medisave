Generics, explained

Good questions deserve clear answers.

Products with the same active ingredient can still differ in strength, dosage form, pack size, and other details. Ask a pharmacist before choosing or changing a medicine.

How should I compare a generic medicine?

Check the active ingredient, strength, dosage form, and pack size on the label. Ask a pharmacist whether the product is suitable for your prescription before switching.

Why can the pill look different?

Color, shape, and inactive ingredients can differ. If you have allergies or a medicine that needs close monitoring, ask your pharmacist or prescriber about switching.

Can MediSave tell me to change my medicine?

No. MediSave provides sample comparison information only. Your prescriber and pharmacist can advise whether a generic or pharmacy choice is right for you.
